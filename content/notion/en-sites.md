# English sites — content outline (Notion 內容清單 英文三表 + 護理學院架構 §四)

Exported 2026-09-29. Audiences (院方指定): international scholars, partner institutions, visiting faculty and exchange students. **No international degree admissions**: no Admissions, International Applicants, tuition, visa, forms or internal calendars on any English site.

Shared rules (架構 §四「英文三站共用規則」):
- 中文／English switches to the SAME unit, never the university home page; the three English sites link to each other clearly.
- The College English site holds the master Faculty Directory, college-wide research and international collaboration; Department and Institute sites summarise + link.
- International Collaboration shows outcomes only; visits, visiting scholars and collaboration inquiries live in Visit and Collaborate (college) or the units' visit/collaboration pages.
- English news is chosen per audience; do not translate scholarship results, internal forms or low-relevance admin notices. Check language, links, titles, degrees and alt text line by line.
- Localise, don't translate: plain global English, no internal jargon/abbreviations (explain 軍陣護理 as "military nursing"; N7x cohort codes must be explained or dropped).
