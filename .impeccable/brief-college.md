## Direction contract

THESIS: The only military nursing college in Taiwan speaks through its uniform's identity system. Every unit is an embroidered patch, every section heading is a white name tape sewn onto twill, the quick entrances are a ribbon bar. Refuses the category default of photo hero + three equal cards + news list, and refuses the pharmacy page's emoji card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Morandi twill. Oatmeal twill ground (#EDE7DE with a fine 45° weave), off-white name tape (#F6F3EE) with deep-sage thread type (#3F5A4E), sage patch cloth (#8FA69A), dusty-pink (#D9B8B3) and misty-blue (#B9C6CC) unit cloths, deep-rose thread (#8C5A5A) for the one primary action per view. Patches: thick merrowed border in thread colour, dashed stitch line inset, shield or rounded-rect silhouette. Depth only by overlapping flat cloth layers offset on the 8px grid; no drop shadows, no glow, no gradient decoration (stripes and weave are the only gradients, as material).

STORY: A visitor sees in words, first, that this is where military nurses are made; recognises the uniform language as authentic to the school; picks their own patch (applicant, parent, student, staff) or a ribbon; lands on a page whose name tapes read like a uniform, plain and orderly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: left 7/12 — name tape "國防醫學大學 護理學院" then the positioning line at 40px/900 in deep-sage thread, a two-sentence paragraph, one deep-rose primary action (招生專區) and one quiet text link (認識本院). Mobile: tape, headline, paragraph, action, then patch at 60% width, ribbons wrap 3+2.
