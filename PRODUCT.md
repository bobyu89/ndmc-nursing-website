# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML fragments generated by a zero-dependency Python 3.12 build (`tokens.py`, `components.py`, `pages/*.py`, `build.py`). Output is pasted into the university CMS "html編輯功能" editor (DevExpress ASPxHtmlEditor) for each page node. The CMS server strips `<style>`, `<svg>`, `<section>/<article>/<details>`; it keeps inline `style`, `class`, `id`, `<img>` (external URL or `data:` URI). The public site loads Bootstrap 5.0.2, so its grid/utility classes are the only responsive mechanism. See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-14-nursing-site-design-system.md` §2.

## Users

Chinese site (院方指定): primary — high-school applicants (mostly on phones, comparing schools, deciding whether a military nursing career fits them), their parents (safety, public-funded tuition, service obligation, career security), and current students (task-driven: forms, dates, rules). Secondary — faculty and staff (evaluation, faculty qualification review, room booking).

English sites (later phase): international scholars and partner institutions; exchange/visiting students. No international degree admissions.

## Product Purpose

The public website of the College of Nursing, National Defense Medical University (國防醫學大學護理學院), which oversees the Department of Nursing (護理學系) and the Graduate Institute of Nursing (護理研究所). The college site is the front door: it states what the college is, routes visitors to the right unit, and gathers cross-unit content (full faculty directory, research overview, international collaboration, admissions overview, college administration).

Success: a first-time applicant or parent understands within one screen what makes this college different and reaches admissions, programs, or career information in two clicks; a student or staff member reaches their form or rule without wading through recruitment content.

## Positioning

The only nursing college in Taiwan that trains military nurses: public-funded education, commissioning as an officer, military nursing (軍陣護理) and trauma/disaster nursing (戰傷與災難護理) as core specialties, clinical training with Tri-Service General Hospital, and a lineage traced to General Chow Mei-yu (周美玉將軍). No other nursing school can truthfully claim this combination.

## Operating Context

- Content lives inside the university's fixed site chrome (top nav, left TreeView menu, footer); only the central content column (~800px desktop, full width on mobile) is ours.
- Content is maintained by college staff through the CMS; named owners per page are recorded in the Notion 內容清單 table (e.g. 院窗口, 三長, 哲君, 教發, 圖儀).
- Planning documents live in the Notion workspace 護理學院workspace → 護理學院網站 → 網站相關分析 (護理學院架構, 受眾需求評估, UI/UX 評估準則, 內容清單).
- Reference the college explicitly benchmarked: the College of Pharmacy page (https://wwwndmc.ndmutsgh.edu.tw/unit/100189/6657) — its audience-routed structure is admired, its card-grid-plus-emoji look is rejected as "too AI".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Information architecture is fixed by 護理學院架構: 12 first-level items (護理學院, 最新消息, 認識本院, 學術單位, 學術資源, 招生專區, 國際交流, 院務與規章, 捐款專區, English, 聯絡我們, 常用連結), max depth two.
- News lists and file-download pages are CMS modules, not html pages; this build designs only html編輯功能 pages, including the landing/overview pages that route into module pages.
- No JavaScript, no `<style>` blocks, no inline SVG, no collapsible elements.
- Must meet the government web accessibility standard (WCAG 2.x AA): text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, alt text on every image, no emoji as icons, no auto-rotating carousels.
- Undecided: how much detail on public-funded tuition, service obligation, commissioning and assignment the college may publish (possible confidentiality or university-level publishing limits). Draft copy on these topics must be marked 待確認.

## Brand Commitments

- Colour direction chosen by the user: Morandi palette, "warm ground, cool primary" — oatmeal/off-white ground, sage green or misty blue as primary, dusty pink as accent.
- Illustration direction chosen by the user: CocoMaterial hand-drawn illustrations (CC0), recoloured to the palette. Photos and illustrations are left as placeholders in this phase.
- Tone: professional and warm; avoid internal jargon (軍護簡介, 杏網相連, N76).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: IA and per-page content outlines (Notion); existing CMS pages (軍護簡介, 師資介紹, 教育理念, 歷史沿革 materials, 2025-2026 annual report referenced as source for 學院簡介); existing carousel photos of exchanges (Northwestern visit, N75 to University of Washington) — not yet collected.
- Absent: photographs, dean's portrait and message, statistics, award lists, MOU partner list, alumni outcome data. Never fabricate numbers, partner names, awards or quotes; write placeholders or 待確認 drafts instead.

## Product Principles

1. Route by audience first: applicants, parents, students, staff and international visitors each find their own entrance without reading everyone else's content.
2. Say what we are in words on the first screen; photographs support the text, never replace it.
3. One source per fact: the college holds the master faculty directory, research overview and international data; units link to it.
4. Plain words over internal names; every link label says where it goes.
5. Built for the phone first, and maintainable by staff who paste text, not by designers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.x AA (Taiwan government accessibility rules). Screen-reader friendly headings, meaningful link text, alt text on all images, 4.5:1 text contrast, touch targets ≥ 44px.
